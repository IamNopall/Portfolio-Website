## Design Specificity Verdict

The architectural monograph format, real summit photography, and telemetry cards give it strong gravity and authentic identity rather than looking like a generic template.

**Deterministic Scan**: Found 14 advisory warnings in `resources/js/views/ProjectDetailView.vue` regarding arbitrary font sizes (`text-[10px]`, `text-[9px]`, `text-[8.5rem]`) falling outside the documented `DESIGN.md` typographic ramp.

## Overall Impression

A confident, bespoke editorial monograph portfolio with exceptional visual weight and atmosphere. The kinetic hierarchy and dark obsidian framing work well; unifying typography classes and tuning mobile spacing will bring it to publication grade.

## What's Working

1. **Architectural Monograph Layout**: The 12-column asymmetric grid and dossier photographic container feel grounded, serious, and uniquely tailored to an engineer.
2. **Atmospheric Contrast**: Deep obsidian canvas (#09090b) paired with crisp hairlines and selective cobalt (#2563eb) accents creates high-contrast legibility.
3. **Live Telemetry & Availability**: Functional status indicators provide immediate credibility without artificial fluff.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Font Size Fragmentation & Type Ramp Deviations**: Multiple arbitrary `text-[10px]`, `text-[9px]`, `text-[8.5rem]` arbitrary values scattered across `ProjectDetailView.vue` instead of standard `DESIGN.md` type ramp classes.
  - *Why it matters*: Visual inconsistency across breakpoints and harder system maintenance.
  - *Fix*: Standardize micro-labels and massive displays to documented design tokens.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable typeset`
- **[P1] Mobile Spacing & Telemetry Stack Density**: On small screens (<640px), dense telemetry cards stack closely with minimal touch breathing room.
  - *Why it matters*: Distracted or mobile users face visual fatigue on smaller viewports.
  - *Fix*: Add dedicated responsive vertical padding and flexible gap rhythm.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Focus Indicators on Interactive Cards**: Keyboard focus states on certain interactive dossier cards could be clearer for accessible navigation.
  - *Why it matters*: Keyboard-only users need high-contrast focus rings when tabbing.
  - *Fix*: Add explicit `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-blue-500` utilities.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable audit`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (Engineering Lead / First-Timer)**: Wants to see clear architectural case study breakdowns with concrete technical challenge/solution metrics; case study details should highlight system architecture diagrams or code snippets prominently.
- **Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: Interactive cards need clear focus-visible outlines against the dark obsidian background when navigating via keyboard.
