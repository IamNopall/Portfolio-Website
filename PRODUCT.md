# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical recruiters, prospective engineering managers, and potential clients seeking an accomplished software engineer and creative developer who bridges robust backend systems with world-class frontend craft.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the definitive digital monograph and living portfolio for Naufal Tsaqif—demonstrating end-to-end full-stack engineering excellence, creative interaction design, and in-depth architectural case studies alongside a bespoke content management console.

## Positioning

Bridging rigorous backend systems engineering (Laravel, relational schema design, REST APIs) with high-craft design engineering and kinetic interaction (Vue 3, GSAP differential scrub, bespoke dark-mode editorial aesthetics) that standard template portfolios cannot replicate.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated by tech recruiters on tight time budgets (requiring instant scannability of capabilities and tech stacks).
- Investigated by engineering leads and clients reviewing deep architectural decisions and case study breakdowns.
- Maintained by Naufal via the dedicated admin management dashboard (`/adminnopal`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Capabilities**:
  - Immersive editorial portfolio home featuring kinetic GSAP scroll animations, typography-driven spatial grid, and capability indexes.
  - Rich project case study views (`/projects/:slug`) detailing challenge, architecture, key features, and live demos.
  - Protected admin console (`/adminnopal`) with PIN access, modal confirmation flows, and project editor.
- **Technical Constraints**:
  - Laravel backend serving a Vue 3 Single Page Application (SPA) compiled via Vite and styled with Tailwind CSS v4.
  - Must deliver fluid 60fps desktop animations with performant, touch-friendly degradation on mobile viewports.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name & Persona**: Naufal Tsaqif — Informatics Engineering, Software Engineer & Creative Developer based in Indonesia.
- **Voice**: Editorial, technically rigorous, confident, concise, and authentic.
- **Visual Palette**: Deep obsidian backdrops, atmospheric spotlights, clean monochrome typography, with cobalt/blue focal accents.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project case studies seeded in `database/seeders/ProjectCaseStudySeeder.php` and `resources/js/data/projectsData.js`.
- Standee profile imagery in `resources/images/guwe.jpeg` (and `public/images/guwe.jpeg`).
- Fully functional Vue 3 views, modals, and route definitions in `resources/js/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Substance Backed by Craft**: Kinetic motion, atmosphere, and visual drama serve to elevate real technical depth, never to conceal shallow work.
2. **Editorial Monograph Feel**: High typographic density, intentional negative space, and disciplined hierarchy over cookie-cutter templates.
3. **Flawless Fluidity**: GSAP interactions and transitions must feel weightless, responsive, and rock-solid across screen sizes.
4. **Autonomous Control**: The platform is a complete software product with its own operational admin tier, not a static read-only brochure.
